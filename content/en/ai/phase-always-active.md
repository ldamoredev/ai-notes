# Always Active

Some habits are not phases. Keep research notes, reference registries, and repeatable playbooks alive while the rest of the atlas changes.

## Operating Memory

- Capture claims separately from conclusions.
- Keep experiments small and reproducible.
- Promote stable procedures into playbooks.
