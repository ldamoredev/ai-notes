# Labs, Research and Playbooks — Always Active

Some work is not a prerequisite phase. Glassbox milestones, research logs, claim verification, source registries, and operational playbooks run across the entire curriculum.

## Mental model

Knowledge stays reliable through a loop of claim, experiment, evidence, review, procedure, and re-verification. Labs and playbooks are the executable memory of that loop.

## Roadmap and operating memory

- Capture evidence, hypotheses, conclusions, and uncertainty separately.
- Keep experiments bounded, seeded, versioned, and reproducible.
- Record negative results and deviations from source methods.
- Promote stable procedures into playbooks with checks and rollback.
- Re-verify changing claims on an explicit cadence.
