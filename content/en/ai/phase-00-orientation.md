# Phase 00 Orientation

The goal of orientation is to know where you are in the map: concept, model behavior, engineering surface, evaluation, or operating practice.

## Outcomes

- Know the difference between learning AI theory and shipping an AI feature.
- Recognize when a question belongs to data, model, prompt, retrieval, product, or evaluation.
- Build a personal glossary before chasing implementation details.
