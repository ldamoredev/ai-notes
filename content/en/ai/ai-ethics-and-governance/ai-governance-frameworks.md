# AI governance frameworks

AI governance frameworks help organizations manage AI risks systematically instead of
through ad hoc reviews. They create roles, processes, evidence, and feedback loops
across the AI lifecycle.

## Framework map

| Framework | Useful lens |
|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF | voluntary risk management across Govern, Map, Measure, Manage |
| NIST Generative AI Profile | genAI-specific risks and actions |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | AI management system requirements and continual improvement |
| OECD AI Principles | human-centered values and policy principles |
| EU AI Act | legal risk tiers and obligations in EU contexts |
| Internal policy | organization-specific approval, ownership, and escalation |

## NIST AI RMF functions

- Govern: policies, roles, culture, accountability, and risk strategy.
- Map: context, stakeholders, impacts, and intended use.
- Measure: metrics, testing, evaluation, and monitoring.
- Manage: prioritize, mitigate, respond, and improve.

## ISO/IEC 42001 lens

ISO/IEC 42001 is a management system standard. It focuses on establishing, operating,
maintaining, and continually improving organizational processes for responsible AI use
and development.

## Pitfall

A framework is not evidence by itself. Auditors, regulators, users, and internal risk
owners need artifacts: inventories, risk decisions, evals, logs, approvals, and reviews.
